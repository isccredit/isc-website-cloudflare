CreditXpert expertise, with ISC doing the work

CreditXpert is a mortgage credit optimization platform that uses predictive analytics and simulation tools to model potential credit score outcomes and identify paths toward a target score. ISC adds a hands-on service layer: instead of asking your team to learn another platform, your ISC support team runs the simulation for you.

ISC has more than 20 years of experience working with CreditXpert. That experience matters when a file is close to qualifying, a pricing threshold is in reach, or you need to compare several possible credit actions before deciding what to pursue.

Typical turnaround: Most CreditXpert simulation requests are completed by ISC in about 30 minutes. Turnaround can vary based on file complexity and the scenarios being reviewed.

What a CreditXpert simulation can help evaluate

Depending on the borrower's credit profile and the scenarios supported by the platform, CreditXpert can help model the potential effect of specific credit changes before money is spent on bureau rescore requests.

Balances

Revolving balance changes

Model how eligible changes to reported revolving balances or utilization may affect the projected mortgage credit score.

Targets

Target-score scenarios

Evaluate potential paths toward a specific score threshold used for qualification, pricing, or a loan-program requirement.

What-if

Specific credit scenarios

Compare supported what-if scenarios to see which documented changes may have the strongest projected impact on the file.

Planning

Rescore planning

Use projected results to help decide whether an eligible credit change may be worth pursuing before bureau rescore fees are incurred.

The simulation helps you evaluate potential options first. If the borrower completes an eligible action and the required documentation is available, a Rapid Rescore may then be used to submit the verified change for bureau review.

What CreditXpert simulation does not do

  • It does not guarantee a score increase. Projected outcomes are estimates and the actual score depends on the borrower's full credit profile and the scoring model in use.
  • It does not directly change bureau data. Any credit-report update must follow the applicable creditor, repository, and documentation process.
  • It does not replace required documentation. A projected scenario is not proof that an account has changed.
  • It is not consumer credit repair. ISC provides mortgage credit simulation support to mortgage professionals as part of the lending workflow.
Current scoring-model note: ISC's current CreditXpert simulation service is used with Classic FICO mortgage score workflows. VantageScore 4.0 and FICO 10T simulations are not currently offered through ISC's simulation tools. Availability may change as vendor platforms evolve.
