Products & Services

Mortgage credit products for every stage of your pipeline.

ISC provides the full range of mortgage credit products on one account, one invoice, and one support team that knows your workflow. From the first soft pull through post-close monitoring, it is all here.

One account. One invoice. One support team.

Most lenders juggle several credit and verification vendors. ISC consolidates the products mortgage professionals actually use, so your team makes one call and keeps files moving.

Credit Reports

Instant 📊

Trended Mortgage Credit Reports

Tri-merge reports with 24-month trended data, ready for Fannie Mae DU and Freddie Mac LP. Classic FICO included, VantageScore 4.0 opt-in available.

AUS Soft Pull

Prequalify borrowers at the earliest stage of the conversation. No hard inquiry, no score impact, instant results. Also known as Soft Pull Plus or Power Profile Plus.

Learn more → Cost-efficient 🎯

SmartSelect Cascading Pulls

Start with one bureau, add the rest only when the borrower passes your filters. The cascade stops when a frozen bureau is detected, helping prevent additional bureau attempts and unnecessary charges.

Borrower Verifications
