Frequently asked questions

How fast do supplements come back?

Often completed within an hour. Call 800-290-2801 for time-critical files. Rush processing is available based on order timing and bureau capacity.

My borrower's credit is frozen. What do I do?

ISC does not handle credit freeze or unfreeze requests. The borrower must contact each bureau directly to lift the freeze, then notify you so you can repull the report.

Bureau freeze contacts:

  • TransUnion: 800-916-8800  ·  transunion.com
  • Equifax: 800-203-7843  ·  equifax.com
  • Experian: 888-397-3742  ·  experian.com

Once all three bureaus are unfrozen, call ISC at 800-290-2801 or repull directly through the MeridianLink portal.

Are ISC's reports compatible with VantageScore 4.0?

VantageScore 4.0 is available for approved lender use in a limited rollout/interim implementation. Classic FICO remains standard for lenders that have not been approved for VantageScore 4.0 use. Availability depends on investor, GSE approval, and platform support.

How do I set up ISC in my LOS?

Search for "Information Searching Company" or "ISC Credit" in your LOS vendor directory. Enter your ISC credentials when prompted. Your ISC support team can help complete many LOS connections quickly once onboarding and account setup are complete. Full setup guide at isccredit.com/integrations/.

How do I request a rapid rescore?

Call 800-290-2801. Your ISC support team will review the credit report with you, discuss rescore strategy (including credit simulation if helpful), and place the order. Standard turnaround is 2-5 business days, with a 24-hour rush option available for time-sensitive files. Results are not guaranteed.

How long does onboarding take?

Most new clients are fully onboarded within 1 business day following their onsite inspection (required by the credit bureaus). Contact ISC to schedule your inspection and start the process, we move as fast as you do.

What are ISC's hours of operation?

Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Pacific Time. For urgent after-hours issues, please leave a message or follow the direct instructions provided by your ISC representative. Standard support requests are handled during business hours.

Can I order ISC products outside of business hours?

Yes, orders can be placed through your LOS integration or client portal 24/7. Orders placed outside business hours will be processed when the team arrives in the morning. For urgent orders, call 800-290-2801. Supplements are often completed within an hour.
